SYSTEM: DANDELION COTTON-GIN (MECHANICAL WORKFLOW)
MODE: intake → separation → cleaning → alignment → densification → spinning → curing

────────────────────────────────────────
1. INPUT MODULE — SEED HEAD INTAKE
────────────────────────────────────────
Function:
- Receive mature dandelion seed heads (white puffballs).
- Feed them gently into the system without crushing.

Key features:
- Gravity-fed hopper or soft vacuum intake.
- Low mechanical stress on pappus fibers.

────────────────────────────────────────
2. SEPARATION MODULE — PAPPUS/SEED SPLITTER
────────────────────────────────────────
Function:
- Separate pappus fibers from seeds (achenes).

Mechanism:
- Counter-rotating soft brush rollers (low RPM).
- Gentle mechanical agitation.
- Seeds fall through mesh; fibers remain suspended.

Outputs:
- Stream A: free pappus fibers.
- Stream B: clean seeds (collected separately).

────────────────────────────────────────
3. CLEANING MODULE — DEBRIS REMOVAL
────────────────────────────────────────
Function:
- Remove dust, broken fibers, and plant fragments.

Mechanism:
- Low-velocity micro-cyclone airflow.
- Fine mesh sieve.
- Optional anti-static treatment.

Output:
- Clean fiber cloud ready for alignment.

────────────────────────────────────────
4. ANTI-STATIC GRID
────────────────────────────────────────
Function:
- Neutralize electrostatic charge on fibers.

Mechanism:
- Fibers pass through conductive or semi-conductive grid.
- Reduces clumping and improves alignment.

────────────────────────────────────────
5. ALIGNMENT MODULE — LAMINAR FLOW CHANNEL
────────────────────────────────────────
Function:
- Orient fibers into a parallel ribbon.

Mechanism:
- Narrow laminar airflow channel.
- Gentle guiding surfaces.
- Fibers align along flow direction.

Output:
- Continuous aligned fiber ribbon.

────────────────────────────────────────
6. COMPRESSION MODULE — DENSIFICATION ROLLERS
────────────────────────────────────────
Function:
- Compress aligned fibers into a dense ribbon.

Mechanism:
- Soft-surface compression rollers.
- Light pressure to remove air pockets.
- Optional humidity mist for cohesion.

Output:
- High-density fiber ribbon suitable for spinning.

────────────────────────────────────────
7. SPINNING MODULE — TWIST SPINDLE
────────────────────────────────────────
Function:
- Spin ribbon into thread.

Mechanism:
- Spindle applies twist under controlled tension.
- Ribbon fed continuously from compression module.
- Optional binder mist (dandelion gel) during twist.

Output:
- Cotton-like dandelion thread.

────────────────────────────────────────
8. TENSION & CURING MODULE
────────────────────────────────────────
Function:
- Maintain tension and set twist.

Mechanism:
- Tension arm holds thread under load.
- Steam or humidity exposure.
- Warm drying to cure binder and lock twist.

Output:
- Finished cotton-like dandelion thread.

────────────────────────────────────────
FLOW SUMMARY
────────────────────────────────────────
Seed heads
 → Intake hopper
 → Soft brush separator (pappus/seed split)
 → Cyclone cleaner
 → Anti-static grid
 → Laminar alignment channel
 → Compression rollers
 → Ribbon output
 → Twist spindle
 → Tension arm + curing
 → Cotton-like dandelion thread

────────────────────────────────────────
3D-PRINTABLE DANDELION COTTON-GIN — DESIGN SPEC
────────────────────────────────────────

MODULES (3D-PRINTABLE)
────────────────────────────────────────
1. Intake Hopper
- Funnels seed heads into separator.
- 3D print: PLA.
- Wall thickness: 1.6 mm.
- Approx. size: 120×80×60 mm.
- Draft angle: ~15° for easy flow.

2. Soft Brush Separator Cylinders
- Counter-rotating cylinders with flexible bristles.
- 3D print cores: PLA.
- 3D print bristle rings: TPU.
- Cylinder size: Ø40 mm × 80 mm.
- Mounted on bearings and threaded rods.

3. Cyclone Cleaner
- Removes dust and broken fibers.
- 3D print: PLA.
- Body size: Ø60 mm × 140 mm.
- Layer height: 0.2 mm.

4. Anti-Static Grid
- Reduces electrostatic clinging.
- 3D print: PETG.
- Lattice thickness: 0.8 mm struts.
- Sized to match channel cross-section.

5. Laminar Alignment Channel
- Aligns fibers into a ribbon.
- 3D print: PLA.
- Dimensions: 120 mm length × 8 mm width × 6 mm height.
- Smooth internal surfaces.

6. Compression Rollers
- Densify fiber ribbon.
- 3D print shafts: PLA.
- 3D print sleeves: TPU (soft surface).
- Roller size: Ø20 mm × 80 mm.
- Mounted on 608 bearings + M5 rods.

7. Ribbon Output Tray
- Collects aligned ribbon before spinning.
- 3D print: PLA.
- Wall thickness: 1 mm.
- Sized to sit under compression rollers.

8. Twist Spindle
- Spins ribbon into thread.
- 3D print: PLA.
- Size: Ø10 mm × 120 mm.
- Optional motor mount for DC motor.

9. Tension Arm
- Holds thread under tension during twist and curing.
- 3D print: PETG.
- Thickness: 3 mm.
- Length: ~140 mm.

────────────────────────────────────────
ASSEMBLY ORDER
────────────────────────────────────────
1. Print all PLA structural parts (hopper, cyclone, channel, tray, spindle).
2. Print TPU brush rings and roller sleeves.
3. Print PETG anti-static grid and tension arm.
4. Assemble soft brush separator:
   - Slide TPU rings onto PLA cylinder cores.
   - Mount cylinders on 608 bearings with M5 rods.
   - Install above hopper outlet.
5. Attach cyclone cleaner to separator output.
6. Install anti-static grid after cyclone.
7. Mount laminar alignment channel downstream of grid.
8. Install compression rollers over channel exit:
   - TPU sleeves on PLA shafts.
   - Shafts on bearings with M5 rods.
9. Place ribbon output tray under compression rollers.
10. Mount twist spindle after tray:
    - On bearings, aligned with ribbon path.
    - Optional motor attached to spindle.
11. Install tension arm downstream of spindle to hold thread under load.
12. Test manual feed:
    - Seed heads into hopper.
    - Observe separation, cleaning, alignment, compression, spinning.
13. Adjust clearances and speeds as needed.
